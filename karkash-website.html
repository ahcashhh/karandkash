About Us

A Professional Wholesale Distribution Company

Karkash Enterprises Inc. is a registered Canadian corporation based in Burnaby, British Columbia. We operate as a B2B wholesale distributor, sourcing quality products directly from brands and manufacturers and supplying them to businesses across Canada and the United States.

We work with brands and suppliers looking for reliable, professional distribution partners who understand the North American market.

Registered Corporation

Provincially incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Full legal entity with business banking and verifiable credentials.

Direct Brand Sourcing

We purchase directly from manufacturers and authorized brand representatives — no intermediaries.

North American Reach

We distribute across Canada and the United States, serving retailers, online sellers, and wholesale buyers.
